Venue

Venue to be announced

We are scouting venues for the next edition. The location will be announced on our Discord as soon as it is confirmed.

What we look for in a venue:

  • High-end tournament PCs
  • High-refresh-rate monitors
  • On-site accommodation
  • Kitchen and lounge areas
  • Dedicated tournament space
  • Good travel connections within Europe

Our 2025 and 2026 editions were hosted at 1337 Camp Gaming House in Waiblingen, near Stuttgart.

Frequently Asked Questions

When and where is the event?

The next edition is in planning. Dates and location are still to be announced – join our Discord to hear it first.

What equipment is provided?

Exact specs depend on the venue and will be confirmed with the announcement. Previous editions ran high-end PCs on high-refresh-rate monitors. Preferably bring your own peripherals (mouse, keyboard, headset).

Where can I find the rules?

For the latest rules, please check our Discord #rules and #info (formats, maps, pick/ban, any changes) channels: discord.gg/u8Ttp8frqU

Do I need a team for CTF?

No - CTF uses a draft system to balance teams. Captains will pick players for their teams.

What's the minimum age?

16+ (under 18 may need guardian consent depending on venue policy).

Where do I sign up?

Registration is not open yet. When it opens it will be announced on our Discord first.

Can I attend without playing?

There is a possibility for spectators. For details, check on our Discord.

Is accommodation included?

Previous editions offered on-site accommodation with various room options. Whether that is available next time depends on the venue – details will follow with the announcement.

What's the skill level requirement?

Join our Discord server to find out details. For CTF there is a minimal threshold of knowledge and experience needed (nr. of games on HOQ pickup and organizer assesment).

Will there be streaming/VODs?

Selected matches may be streamed on twitch.tv/qllancityseries. Links will be posted in Discord.

What about food and drinks?

Previous venues had a kitchen and lounge area with restaurants and shops nearby. Catering details will follow with the venue announcement.
