Privacy Notice

What appears on this site, and how to get it removed

The QL City LAN Series is a community event run by volunteers. We keep this short and in plain language, because that is more useful to you than legal boilerplate. If anything here is unclear, ask us – contact details are at the bottom.

Who we are

The QL City LAN Series is a community-run Quake Live LAN event organised by players, for players. We are the ones responsible for the information described on this page.

What this site collects

Nothing. There are no forms on this site, so there is nothing to fill in and nothing gets sent to us by visiting. We use no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies, and no third-party scripts run on these pages.

If registration opens for a future edition, this page will be updated to describe exactly what that collects before it goes live.

Nicknames, photos and video

LAN events are social and public by nature. If you take part:

  • Your nickname may appear in brackets, match results, the results page on this site, and in Discord announcements
  • Photos and videos taken at the event may be published on this website, in our Discord server, and in event media such as Twitch streams, VODs and highlight videos

If you would rather not appear in shared photos or video, tell us – message the organisers on Discord and we will do our best to keep you out of published media. Tournament results and brackets will normally still list your nickname, since they are the public record of the competition.

How long it stays up

Results, brackets, photos and videos stay published as the archive of each event, unless you ask us to take something down.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to take a photo, video or nickname down from this site or our Discord, or to correct something we have got wrong. Just ask – you do not need to give a reason, and we will not make it difficult.

Contact

For anything on this page, including removal requests, email or message the organisers on our Discord.
